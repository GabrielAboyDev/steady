# Dictionary data sources

`en.sdict` is compiled by `scripts/build-dictionary.sh` from these public datasets.
Confirm the licenses suit your distribution before shipping.

## Word frequencies

- **FrequencyWords** by hermitdave — `content/2018/en/en_50k.txt`
  https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
- Derived from the OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus (http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles.php).
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC-BY-SA 4.0).

## Bigrams

- **count_2w.txt** from Peter Norvig's n-gram collection — https://norvig.com/ngrams/
- Derived from the Google Web Trillion Word Corpus (Thorsten Brants and Alex Franz,
  distributed by the Linguistic Data Consortium). Norvig's compact extracts are
  published for free use; the full LDC corpus has its own terms.
