Steady

A keyboard that reshapes itself as you type

Steady is an iOS keyboard built for people who type on a touchscreen but miss keys — tremor, limited dexterity, single-finger or switch use. As you type, it enlarges the letters most likely to come next and shrinks or removes the ones that cannot follow, so your tap targets grow as the word narrows.

Everything runs on your device. Steady makes no network calls and never asks for “Allow Full Access”. Nothing you type leaves your iPhone or iPad.

Setting up the keyboard

  1. Open the Steady app.
  2. Go to Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard and choose Steady.
  3. In any app, press and hold the globe key (🌐) to switch to Steady.

You do not need to grant Full Access. Steady works entirely without it.

Choosing how much the keys resize

Open the Steady app and go to Settings. There are three profiles:

  • Off — keys stay a fixed size; predictions still appear in the suggestion bar.
  • Medium — unlikely letters shrink and fade.
  • Aggressive — impossible letters are removed from the row entirely.

Rows keep a fixed height and keys never reorder, so a key you are reaching for will not move out from under you.

Names and phrases

  • Names — saved entries that insert exactly as written, for proper nouns that prediction will never guess.
  • Phrases — sentence templates with fill-in-the-blank slots. A name slot draws from your Names list. Picking a phrase walks you through each blank with large chips, then inserts the finished sentence.

Both are edited in the Steady app and become available in the keyboard immediately.
